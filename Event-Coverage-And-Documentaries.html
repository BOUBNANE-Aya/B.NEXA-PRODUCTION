Award-Winning Documentary

Excellence Center

We collaborated with the Centre d’Excellence de Kénitra, part of the Licence d’Excellence in Audiovisual and Game Design program, to create a documentary that highlights the spirit and creativity of the school.

This project was a proud achievement for us, as the documentary was selected for the First-Time Filmmaker Sessions – Volume 9, gaining international recognition and showcasing the talent nurtured within the program.

Impact Documentary

SOQYA Association

We worked with Soqya Association to create a documentary that sheds light on their impactful mission. The film follows their operations step by step — from identifying areas without access to clean water, to locating sources, and ultimately helping villages gain this vital resource.

Due to the private nature of this project, the documentary cannot be shared publicly. All rights are reserved to Soqya Association.
