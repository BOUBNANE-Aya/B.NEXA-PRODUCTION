Personal Projects like ROOTED IN LABOR

Alongside our collaborations, we also create independent projects to express our cinematic vision and raise awareness on meaningful topics. One of our films, deeply rooted in the theme of labor, has been selected for festivals such as the British University Film Festival and recognized by several others. For now, we are keeping this project private, as its full richness has yet to reach a worldwide audience. We believe movies lose part of their potential when released too early on social media, which is why we plan to share it publicly next year. In the meantime, here’s a glimpse through its trailer.

COMMERCIAL VIDEOS FOR BDEA

BDEA Carrosserie

We created videos for BDEA, a specialized workshop that takes vehicles with empty interiors and transforms them into fully equipped transport solutions. From installing seats, carpets, and lighting to complete interior finishing, BDEA customizes vehicles for tourism, school, and personal use. We covered both their internal and external events, and some of this content remains unreleased on social media, making it exclusive.

Documentary of Excellence Center

We collaborated with the Centre d’Excellence de Kénitra, part of the Licence d’Excellence in Audiovisual, Game Design, and Interactive Multimedia program, to create a documentary that highlights the spirit and creativity of the school. This project was a proud achievement for us, as the documentary was selected for the First-Time Filmmaker Sessions – Volume 9, gaining international recognition and showcasing the talent nurtured within the program.
