COMMERCIAL VIDEOS FOR BDEA

BDEA Carrosserie

We created videos for BDEA, a specialized workshop that takes vehicles with empty interiors and transforms them into fully equipped transport solutions. From installing seats, carpets, and lighting to complete interior finishing, BDEA customizes vehicles for tourism, school, and personal use.

Documentary of Excellence Center

We collaborated with the Centre d'Excellence de Kénitra to create a documentary that highlights the spirit and creativity of the school. Selected for the First-Time Filmmaker Sessions – Volume 9, gaining international recognition.
